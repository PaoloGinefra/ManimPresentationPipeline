# Stage 7: Rehearsal (optional)

A trial run in front of people, and a plan for their notes.

## Input

The approved draft deck and script, and the reviewers' notes (by slide number).

## Steps

1. Translate each note to its stable ID through the build commit the reviewers saw
   (`feedback.md`).
2. Echo each note back with its stage. The author confirms or corrects.
3. Write a plan: the notes grouped by the earliest stage they touch, in stage order, with the
   fix for each and the time it adds or removes.
4. After the author approves the plan, apply it stage by stage, re-approving only what changed.
5. Record every note, its stage, its fix and its commit in `7-review/log.md`.

If the talk must get shorter, propose whole beats to cut or move to backup, never tighter
wording.

## Checkpoint

The plan.

## Done when

Every note is fixed or declined in the log. Tag `global/rehearsal-1`.
