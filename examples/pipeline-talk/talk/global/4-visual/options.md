# Visual options

Peaks first, then every beat. Level: static, motion or scene. **Picked** marks the choice made by
the agent standing in for the author.

## Beats

### B1. Motion is expensive

1. (motion) A queue of requests fills; a cache box beside it warms from grey to blue. **Picked.**
2. (static) A slide of bullets next to an animated frame.

Suggested: 1. It shows, in five seconds, the kind of idea motion carries.

### B2. Notes arrive late

1. (motion) A ladder of four rungs, outline to deck, widening with cost; note cards drop onto the top rung. **Picked.**
2. (static) A bar chart of the cost of a change by stage.

Suggested: 1. There is no measured cost (digest G1), so no chart with numbers.

### B3. Check where it's cheap

1. (motion) The ladder turns on its side: its rungs become stages of a row, and the missing stages slide in. **Picked.**
2. (static) The nine stages as a table.

Suggested: 1. The rungs and the stages are the same things, so the morph is honest.

### B4. One sentence, every stage

1. (scene) The sentence as a card above the row; each click moves it one stage right and redraws it in that stage's form. **Picked.**
2. (static) Seven small panels side by side.

Suggested: 1. One object, persisting, is the claim: it is the same sentence throughout.

### B5. One source file

1. (motion) The storyboard file in the centre; the script and the deck are drawn out of it. **Picked.**
2. (static) A diagram of files and arrows.

### B6. A late note goes home

1. (scene) A note card appears over the deck end of the row, gets its ID, its stage, travels back to the script stage, and the stages after it light up one by one. **Picked.**

### B7. Using it

1. (static) The repository's folders, with `talk/` highlighted, and the two commands. **Picked.**
2. (motion) A terminal typing the commands.

Suggested: 1. A typing terminal is decoration.

### B8. This slide was a grey box

1. (motion) The closing slide drawn first as grey boxes, then turning into itself. **Picked.**

### S1 (backup). What the build checks

1. (static) Two headlines overlapping, flagged; two seam frames side by side. **Picked.**

## Version 2 (review notes #5 and #7)

Act 2 became one beat per stage. Every stage beat uses one visual, picked without alternatives
because the author asked for this structure: the cache talk's checkpoint on the card, then the card
shrinks left and "the author checks" appears on the right, item by item, and the stage ticks. The
card-changing-form image of version 1 survives as the thread across those beats. Alignment rules are
now part of the skeleton (note #7).
